FAQ

Asked and answered.

Can you write the report under our brand?

Yes. We regularly produce white-label reports in our agency clients' templates.

Do you only report on data you collected?

No. We can analyse and report on any data set, including your own CRM or past studies.

What does a report cost?

It depends on scope. Reporting is quoted separately from fieldwork, usually as a fixed fee per study.
