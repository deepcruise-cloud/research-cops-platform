    Each language version is checked inside the programmed survey before launch.

FAQ

Asked and answered.

Which languages do you cover?

All major European and Asian languages, plus Arabic, Hebrew, Portuguese (BR/PT), Spanish (LATAM/ES), Hindi and other Indian languages. Ask us about any others.

Do you use machine translation?

Only as a starting draft for open-ended responses. Survey text and stimuli are always translated and reviewed by people.

Can you handle right-to-left languages?

Yes. Arabic and Hebrew are programmed and checked with the correct right-to-left layout.
