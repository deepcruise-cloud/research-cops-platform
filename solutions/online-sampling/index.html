FAQ

Asked and answered.

Where does your sample come from?

Our proprietary Opinion Genie panel, plus a network of vetted partner panels. We blend sources and de-duplicate across them, so a respondent can only complete your study once.

Do you work as a white-label supplier?

Yes. Many of our clients are research agencies. We never contact your end client, and all reporting can carry your brand.

What is your minimum project size?

We field studies from 50 completes. Very niche B2B or HCP audiences may need a minimum fee, which we state in the quote.

How do you price sample?

On a cost-per-complete (CPI) basis. CPI depends on audience, country, incidence, LOI and quotas. You pay only for valid completes.
