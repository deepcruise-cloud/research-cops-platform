Length discipline

FAQ

Asked and answered.

Can you review a questionnaire we already wrote?

Yes. A questionnaire audit is a fixed-fee service. We send back tracked changes, the reason for each one, and a revised LOI estimate.

Do you design for mobile respondents?

Always. More than half of respondents now take surveys on a phone, so we avoid large grids and long lists.

Which methodologies do you support?

U&A, brand health, concept and ad testing, pricing (Van Westendorp, Gabor-Granger), MaxDiff, segmentation and customer satisfaction.
