Capabilities

Data Processing & Reporting, feature by feature.

Cleaning rules

Speeder, straight-liner, duplicate, partial, poor open-end and attention-check rules, plus custom rules. Removed completes are tracked.

Weighting

RIM and cell weighting, with trimming and diagnostics, plus derived variables.

Banner tables

Crosstabs, including pooled multi-wave crosstabs, and automated insights.

Open-end quality checks & framework

Poor-quality and gibberish open ends are flagged for review, and answers are organised into a theme framework. Other languages are translated first, and the framework can be exported.

Exports

CSV, SPSS (.sav), Excel banner book and PowerPoint deck, plus MaxDiff and CBC exports.

A report for every method

An on-screen dashboard, and a dedicated report for each methodology.
