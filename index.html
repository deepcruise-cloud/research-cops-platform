The Research COPS Advantage

Built for speed, precision, and scale.

One Platform. Every Research Phase.

Stop juggling five vendors for one study. Research COPS consolidates panel sourcing, survey programming, fieldwork management, data processing, and insight delivery into a single integrated platform. Your project manager becomes your single point of contact — from brief to boardroom. Real-time dashboards give you full visibility into field progress, quota fills, and quality metrics without a single follow-up email.

No Hidden Fees. No Surprise Markups.

Our CPI-based model means you only pay for verified completes. B2B studies start from just $7 per interview for standard targeting parameters. Every line item is visible upfront — panel costs, programming fees, and processing charges. No ambiguous "platform fees" or post-project adjustments. You quote it, you scope it, you pay exactly that.

From 50 Respondents to 50,000+

From 50-respondent pilot studies to 50,000+ multi-country trackers. Our infrastructure scales elastically with your research needs — without compromising data quality or turnaround times. Spin up new geographies in hours, not weeks. Add quotas mid-field without restarting. Our architecture was built for the reality of modern research: fast pivots, tight deadlines, and zero tolerance for bad data.

Enterprise-Grade Fraud Prevention

Every response passes through our multi-layered security stack before it counts as a complete. We don't just catch bots — we eliminate sophisticated fraud patterns that other platforms miss.

Verification

Geo-Location & IP Verification

Real-time geographic validation ensures respondents are where they claim to be. Duplicate IPs are flagged and blocked instantly.

Anti-Fraud

Proxy/VPN Detection & Blocking

Advanced detection algorithms identify and filter respondents routing traffic through anonymous proxies, VPNs, or Tor exit nodes.

Quality

Speeder Analysis & Bot Filtering

Response-timing algorithms and behavioral analysis detect automated bots, straight-liners, and speed-clickers before they corrupt your dataset.

Identity

Digital Fingerprinting & Deduplication

Browser fingerprinting, device ID tracking, and cross-study deduplication ensure each response comes from a unique, verified human.

Compliance

GDPR & CCPA Compliant Data Handling

Full regulatory compliance with data masking, consent management, and right-to-erasure workflows built into every project by default.
