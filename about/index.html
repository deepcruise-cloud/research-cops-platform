About Research COPS

Research Centric Ops.

We are a market research operations company based in Greater Noida, India, and the team behind the Research Centric OPS platform. We help agencies, brands, startups, advertisers and panel companies collect data they can trust and turn it into decisions.

Our story

Built by researchers who were tired of the seams.

Research COPS began as a fieldwork and survey-operations partner for market research agencies. The work they need sounds simple and is hard to do well: find the right respondents, program the survey correctly, keep the fraud out and deliver on time.

Over time our clients asked us to do more: write the questionnaire, translate it into new markets, process the data and present what it means. We now run the full research lifecycle, either as a white-label team behind an agency or as the research partner for brands and startups without a research department.

Running hundreds of projects taught us where the time and margin really go: in the seams between tools. So we built Research Centric OPS, one platform where the RFQ, the quote, the script, the sample, the data and the report all live in the same project. Alongside it sits Opinion Genie, our own panel, working with a network of API-integrated partner panels.

Our name is our approach: Research Centric Ops. We put the research first, and run the operations behind it with the discipline of a well-run engineering team.
