FAQ

Asked and answered.

Why aren't prices listed?

Plans are priced on modules, seats and AI credits, and many clients combine them with fieldwork. Tell us what you need and we will send exact pricing within one business day.

Is fieldwork included?

No. Fieldwork (sample) is quoted separately, per project, on a cost-per-complete basis.

What are AI credits?

AI features, such as the AI Survey Assistant on Sigma, use AI credits. Each plan includes a monthly allowance: Alpha 200, Beta 500, Gamma 1,500, Sigma 4,000.

How do I get an account?

Register at diy.researchcops.com. New accounts are activated after admin approval.
